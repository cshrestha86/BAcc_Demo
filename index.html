💡 What do these mean?

  • Selling price. Zara charges each customer £4.00 for an iced latte.
  • Cost per cup. Coffee, milk, ice and the cup cost £1.50. This stays the same for every cup. Her total ingredient bill rises as she sells more cups.
  • Fixed costs. Truck rental, the pitch fee and insurance cost £350 a week. She pays this whether she sells 1 cup or 1,000.

Round 4 · Predict

🍬 Round 4: predict the result

Zara picks Option C, and the promo works even better than planned. She now sells 300 cups a week. Her price is still £4.00 and her cost per cup is still £1.50. Fixed costs stay at £420.

Short on time? You can skip this round and go straight to the bonus round.

Prediction 1: What happens to profit when she sells more cups?

🍬🍬 2 candies

Prediction 2: What is Zara’s net profit at 300 cups?

🍬🍬🍬 3 candies

Price £4.00, cost per cup £1.50, fixed costs £420.

Net profit per week = £
💡 Worked answer
Revenue: £4.00 × 300 = £1,200
Variable costs: £1.50 × 300 = £450
Contribution: £1,200 − £450 = £750
Net profit: £750 − £420 = £330 ✅

🔑 The big accounting idea

  • Fixed costs stay at £420 when Zara sells more cups.
  • So each extra cup adds its full £2.50 contribution to profit.
  • Sales rose from 200 to 300 cups, which is 50% more.
  • Profit rose from £80 to £330, which is more than four times as much.
  • Accountants call this operating leverage. It also works in reverse, so profit falls fast when sales drop.

⚡ Running true or false and memory

  • Teams write T or F, or a card number, and hold up their boards.
  • Press Start, then tap each team’s answer in the order the boards go up. That order decides the speed bonus.
  • Press Reveal when every team has answered or the timer ends. Points go to the leaderboard straight away.
  • Tapped the wrong button? Press “Fix an answer”, correct it and reveal again. The speed order stays the same.
  • This works well for up to about 12 teams. For a large crowd answering on their own phones, use Kahoot instead.

✍️ How teams answer

Use mini whiteboards by default. They need no Wi-Fi and no phones. If the room has good Wi-Fi, you can run the questions in Kahoot instead. Use one method, not both, or the room will lose track of the scores.
